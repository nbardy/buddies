Owner feedback (#unleashd-2, thread `post_01a0ee26-b3f1-7506-8ec1-51273329235a`): the cut is

## What is wrong with the current cut (measured from `edit/src/Assembly.tsx`)

1. **Cuts land mid-phrase.** The EDM is in 4-bar phrases (bars 1, 5, 9, 13, 17…). After the drop,
   sections start on bars 6, 12, 15, 18, 26, 28, 29, 33, 35. Each lands on a bar, but only 29 and
   33 are phrase starts. The ear expects change every 4 bars and gets it after 5, 6, 3 and 3.
   That is the "off step."
2. **The music restarts after the title.** Marimba resolves on "Introducing 2.0", marimba plus soft
   hits under the four benefits, then the EDM starts **from nothing** (a muffled half-time kick)
   under someone typing. The energy goes up, down, down, then up again. It's two songs butted
   together.
3. **Too many scenes.** There are 14 sections in 90 s. Inside them, design review has 7 cuts in
   11 s, design iteration 6, and the feature flashes are 1.4 s each. A new viewer never settles
   long enough to learn what they are looking at.
4. **Two demos look the same.** Design review and design iteration are both "thread pane with
   screenshots," back to back. The multimedia demo is meta ("share the latest video" → this video).
   Nobody new can tell the three apart.
5. **Claims repeat without building.** "Multi harness" is said at 18 s, again at 55 s, and again
   at 84 s. Free and Open Source are also said three times each.

## The story in one line

Too many AI apps → one place where your agents work together → you own it.

## The musical story in one line

Tension (pops, riser) → silence + boom → marimba calm → **the marimba melody becomes the build** →
drop into the product → one continuous groove, changing only every 4 bars → breakdown for the
why → final hit → end card. There is one cue, and the only join is the marimba handing its
melody to the build.

## Rules for the edit

- **Scene changes only on phrase starts** (bars 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25, 29, 31). A crash marks each one.
- **At most 3 cuts per scene.** Movement inside a scene comes from slow pushes (4–10%), not cuts.
- **One caption per scene,** same place (lower left), on screen for at least 2 s.
- **Each claim twice:** once as the promise (build), once as the recap (after the ethos). The
  product scenes prove them without saying them again.
- **Cut:** the mid-film Free / Private / Open Source stack (already gone), the design-iteration
  scroll and redesign part (only its contact sheet and "Great work!" stay), and the typing
  half of the multimedia demo (only the inline video stays). Familiar UI goes too; the product
  scenes already show it.
